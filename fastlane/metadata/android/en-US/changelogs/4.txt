1.0.3 changes the typeface.

- Uses the Inter typeface throughout the app, the same one the website uses. The fonts
  are bundled inside the app, so nothing is downloaded and the app still has no
  INTERNET permission.
- The alarm screen that appears when an alarm rings now uses the same typeface as the
  rest of the app.
- On narrow phones, a long date such as the next new moon no longer runs into its
  label. The date now wraps neatly on the right.
