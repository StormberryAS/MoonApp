1.0.1 repairs the first release. Testing v1.0.0 on a phone found the app was wearing
SunApp's icon and had no theme of its own.

Fixed
- The launcher icon was SunApp's sun, byte for byte. It is now MoonApp's crescent, drawn
  from the same favicon the website uses, with a themed-icon layer that survives tinting.
- Typed text was unreadable. The app declared no colour scheme, so Material fell back to
  its light palette and painted near-black text on the dark cards, including the city
  search and both coordinate fields.
- The header ran underneath the status bar and the last alarm's switch sat in the
  home-gesture strip. The app now applies system-bar insets.
- Alarms have their own screen. You can choose any of the five lunar events, set the
  offset, name an alarm, edit it and delete it. Before, one button made a fixed
  "15 minutes before moonrise" alarm that could never be changed or removed.
- An unanswered alarm rang and vibrated until the battery died. It now stops after ten
  minutes, keeps the screen on while it rings, and ignores the back gesture so the
  dismiss button cannot be swiped away.
- Typed coordinates took the phone's timezone instead of the place's, so Sydney's
  coordinates on a Norwegian phone showed Oslo times.
- Moon times are now computed over the place's own day rather than the UTC day, which
  moved rise and set for everywhere east or west of Greenwich.

Added
- Pick any date, not only today.
- Phase name, distance to the moon, and the dates of the next new and full moon.
- Days when the moon stays up or never rises are now told apart, instead of both
  reading "does not occur today".
- A first-run notice saying plainly that this is a prototype, that the figures are
  computed rather than observed, and that an Android alarm is best effort.
